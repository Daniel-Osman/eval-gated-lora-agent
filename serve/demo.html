Eval-gated LoRA agent

A 27B QLoRA adapter, merged and requantized to 4-bit, on one A10. The model writes a plan; the plan runs against a freshly seeded mock shop; a Phase A gate decides whether the reply may be sent. This page shows every step, including where the gate approves a decision that was wrong.

Before the first click: the first request after idle waits about 5 minutes while the model loads.

The container scales to zero after 60 s idle and keeps nothing warm. Cold start is measured at 309 s end to end through this page's path, of which 291.4 s is engine init reported by the container (engine.init_seconds). The split between container start and engine init is not measured. Once warm, a composed request takes 1.5–2.4 s and free text about 6.3 s. Phase A checks that the sentence is true against state, not that the decision was right.

seen means the case was in the training split. unseen means it is in the frozen bench but not in training — a match there is still a compatibility check, not a generalisation claim.

Quality on unseen tickets is unmeasured. The gate checks truth, not wisdom: it checks the reply against the recorded state changes and the claim shapes it recognises. It does not read free prose in general, and it does not judge whether the action was the right one to take.
